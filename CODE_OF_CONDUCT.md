# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful in issues, reviews, and discussions. Disagreement about design is welcome. Insults, harassment, and publishing someone else's private information are not.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository owner. The owner may remove comments, reject contributions, or block further participation. This project follows the spirit of Contributor Covenant 2.1. The full text of that covenant is at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
